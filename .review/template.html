Counterfactual pilot · stage 1

Ten frames, taken one step before the grasp goes wrong

Each frame is the last moment at which a different action could still have saved the attempt. Gemma saw only the image and the same question every time, and had to answer with exactly one of eight action labels. Below: what it proposed, under five phrasings of that question.

    Reference correction is not ground truth. It is the correction a person would call obvious for that injected fault, recorded for eyeballing only and never shown to the model. Green means the answer matched it, red means it did not — that is agreement with a human guess, not accuracy.

    Which labels ever got used

    Across all 50 calls on this page, Gemma reached for five of the eight labels. Three never appeared once — and one of them, OPEN_GRIPPER, is the correction for the gripper_closed_early frames. A proposal set with a hole in it cannot be rescued by choosing more carefully from within it.
